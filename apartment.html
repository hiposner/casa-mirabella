Layout overviewPanoramica degli spazi

Casa Mirabella is a comfortable attic apartment on the third floor of the building, with no elevator. It is best suited to guests who are happy with stairs and want a practical, well-equipped home base for exploring the Dolomites. The apartment has a master bedroom with a queen-size bed, a second bedroom with single beds, one bathroom with a tub/shower, toilet, bidet, and washing machine. There is a compact but highly functional kitchen and a cozy living area for relaxing after a day outside.

The apartment is compact, as many mountain attic apartments are, but it is not tiny. It works very well for a couple, a small family, or four guests who plan to spend their days hiking, skiing, cycling, or exploring. Because this is my own home when guests are not staying here, it is comfortable, thoughtfully set up, and has the things you need for a real stay — It is a home, not a formal hotel.
